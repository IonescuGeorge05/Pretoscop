M20 — founder check (about 20 minutes)
1. Switch the portal on for your local database only when you want to try it: `make psql`, then `UPDATE feature_flags SET enabled = true WHERE key = 'merchants.portal.enabled';` (it stays off by default; turning it off again stops new registrations and submissions, not approved feeds).
2. With `make dev` (worker running), open http://localhost:3000/pentru-magazine → "Înregistrează magazinul". Sign in with an address on a domain you control (or use Mailpit at http://localhost:8025 for the link), fill in the company (a wrong CUI is refused), and watch step 2: an address on the shop's domain verifies it; otherwise follow the DNS TXT or file instructions and press "Verifică acum".
3. Step 3: paste a real compari.ro or Google Merchant feed URL of a shop you work with (or upload a sample CSV). Within a minute the report shows the format, how many products can be listed, the hints ("12.500" ambiguity, missing EAN, delivery, off-domain URLs) and 20 offers exactly as we would show them. Check a few totals against the shop's site.
4. Step 4–5: choose the price format if asked, enter delivery rules if the feed has none, accept the terms, submit. You receive the confirmation email; the team mailbox receives the notification.
5. As admin, open http://localhost:3000/admin/comercianti → the merchant → "Aprobă". The feed source appears in /admin/surse (key `<slug>:feed:merchant`, every 4 hours) and runs once immediately (needs `CRAWL_ENABLED=true` and the `crawl.enabled` flag). Try "Respinge"/"Suspendă" on a second test merchant: the statement of reasons is required and is emailed.
6. Back in the merchant account: the dashboard shows the offers, the matched share, clicks over 30 days, freshness, the last runs and "Ce poți îmbunătăți". Once matching runs, open one of the matched products and see the shop's offer.
7. Read /pentru-magazine/termeni and send it to the lawyer with the other legal pages (B5): feed-accuracy warranty, suspension with reasons, 15 days' notice of changes, complaints within 15 working days.
8. Decide when to open the portal publicly (the flag) and who answers the approval queue (one business day is the promise to merchants).
