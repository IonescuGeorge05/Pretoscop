## 1. Affiliate economics in Romania (2Performant, Profitshare, eMAG access)

### Takeaway
Affiliate cost-per-sale is the fastest revenue line that needs no merchant sales effort.
- **Network averages (2Performant 2025, reported):** about €0.137 earned per click (EPC) over 61.4M clicks, a 3.72% click-to-sale rate, a €60.09 average basket excluding VAT, and affiliate commission equal to 6.11% of sales.
- **By category:** electronics converts at only 2.2% but has a €191 average order, which implies about 3.2% commission. Fashion, beauty, books and health pay an implied 7.7–10.9%.
- **eMAG is the big unknown.** Profitshare stayed in-house at eMAG after the 2Performant takeover lapsed in August 2025. Its public terms do **not** explicitly ban price comparators. They do require every website and promotion method to be declared and approved, and they let advertisers restrict any affiliate. So the teammate's "comparators forbidden" claim is **unverified**: it may sit in eMAG's program-specific rules, which are behind a login.

### Inferences

**Formula.** EPC = conversion rate × AOV × commission rate. Affiliate revenue = click-outs × share of click-outs that can be monetized × EPC × attribution factor.

**Network EPC, computed from 2Performant data:** €0.082 (2023) → €0.104 (2024) → €0.137 (2025), which is about 0.71 RON per click in 2025. EPC rose even as clicks fell, because conversion improved.

**Implied affiliate commission by category, 2025 (estimate = EPC ÷ (conversion × AOV)):**

| Category | Sales per click | EPC | Implied commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| Books | €1.34 | €0.136 | ~10.1% |
| Health & Personal care | €1.14 | €0.124 | ~10.9% |
| Beauty | €1.70 | €0.146 | ~8.6% |
| Fashion | €2.42 | €0.186 | ~7.7% |
| Babies/Toys | €2.22 | €0.158 | ~7.2% |
| Automotive | €2.10 | €0.120 | ~5.7% |
| Pet | €1.74 | €0.096 | ~5.5% |
| Home & Garden | €2.71 | €0.145 | ~5.3% |
| Pharma | €1.57 | €0.072 | ~4.6% |
| Electronics IT&C | €4.20 | €0.136 | ~3.2% |

This answers the brief's guesses: electronics sits at about 3%, inside the guessed 1–4%. Fashion at about 8% is inside the guessed 5–10%. Beauty and books are higher, at roughly 9–10%.

**Comparison traffic is high-intent but leaks.** Comparison click-outs come at the bottom of the funnel, after the user has picked a product and a shop, so conversion could equal or beat the 3.72% network average. Three things erode it:
- On mobile, the hand-off to retailer apps can break the tracking cookie (apps are over 50% of sales for many merchants).
- Under last-click attribution, a later retargeting, cashback or coupon click can overwrite Pretoscop's credit.
- Returns and cancellations remove commissions.

For planning, cut the network EPC by 10–40%, giving €0.08–0.12.

**eMAG monetization options, in order:**
1. Apply to Profitshare, declare the site as a price-comparison and price-history site, and get written approval. This is required anyway under clause 4.8 for any non-standard method.
2. If refused, keep listing eMAG offers **unmonetized**. Completeness is what makes "lowest final price first" credible.
3. Monetize the many eMAG marketplace sellers (15,000+) that also run their own shops on 2Performant or Profitshare.
4. Sell sponsored or CPC placements to other retailers who want to beat eMAG.

eMAG's record 2025 loss could push it either toward performance marketing, consistent with the announced higher commissions, or toward cost cuts. The direction is uncertain.

**eMAG share of click-outs is a key parameter.** 8.71bn lei at 5.2 RON/EUR is about €1.67bn of revenue, roughly 20% of the €8bn+ physical-goods market. That revenue excludes marketplace GMV, so eMAG's share of sales is probably higher. Treat the brief's "40%+" as unverified, but expect eMAG to win a large share of "lowest price" click-outs in electronics. If those clicks are unmonetized, effective EPC falls by the same share.

**Timeline to first revenue (estimate):**
- 2Performant: commissions can be tracked within days of launch, since collaborations are now auto-approved. Cash arrives about 30–75 days later, after merchant approval and the monthly cycle.
- Profitshare: the 22nd-to-5th cycle plus website verification gives a similar lag.

## 2. Merchant CPC listings ("pay per click-out") and the chicken-and-egg problem

### Takeaway
Merchant cost-per-click fees are the core revenue of mature comparators. At Ceneo, price-comparison fees were 86.5% of H1 2025 revenue. Compari.ro prices clicks by category and price band, with a 150 RON minimum top-up and bid multipliers from 1.5 to 100.

Merchants pay only for proven traffic, and the incumbent compari.ro (2,500+ merchants, 2.4–4.3M Romanian active users) is itself shifting to a hybrid marketplace at 3–5.5% commission (since July 2025). So a newcomer should:
1. List all shops free and monetize through affiliate networks first.
2. Give merchants free click and price-position reports.
3. Launch self-serve CPC or "boost" placements only once it can send roughly 25–50k click-outs a month, likely around months 9–12 in a base case.

### Cited Findings

  - It charges per redirect to the merchant's site. The per-click price depends on the main category, sometimes the final subcategory, and the displayed price band, and is published in an XLSX price list. Base click prices exclude VAT. The minimum balance top-up is 150 RON. — [compari.ro pricing information](https://www.compari.ro/static/pricing-information.html) (Cloudflare blocked a direct fetch, so this content comes via search-engine summary)
  - Bidding comes in three modes: base, category and product. The multiplier ranges from 1.5 to 100.0, and 1.0 is also allowed for category and product bids. When modes are combined, the lowest resulting bid applies. — [compari.ro bidding](https://www.compari.ro/static/bidding.html)

Czech press framed the deal as Heureka trying to "improve its reputation among e-shops". — [CEE Legal Matters](https://ceelegalmatters.com/czech-republic/16432-ksb-advises-heureka-group-on-acquisition-of-dataweps); [CC.cz](https://cc.cz/heureka-se-rozhodla-zlepsit-svou-povest-mezi-e-shopy-proto-koupila-cesky-startup-jana-mayera-a-udelala-z-nej-sveho-sefa/)
- **Romanian Google Shopping CPC trend:** an agency advertorial says Shopping CPC in Romania rose almost 27% in 2.5 years and has not returned to 2024 levels. It also notes that a merchant whose products appear on external platforms ends up bidding against its own offers. — [Economica (Webdigital advertorial)](https://www.economica.net/analiza-webdigital-efectele-presiunii-jucatorilor-externi-asupra-pietei-de-ecommerce-din-romania_965837.html)

### Inferences

**A merchant's rational maximum CPC is about conversion × AOV × target cost of sale.** 2Performant merchants pay 8.4% of sales in total. That gives (estimates):
- Electronics: 2.20% × €190.77 × 8.4% ≈ €0.35 per click.
- Fashion: 4.05% × €59.78 × 8.4% ≈ €0.20.
- Books: 5.47% × €24.56 × 8.4% ≈ €0.11.

A newcomer can credibly charge about 0.25–1.0 RON (€0.05–0.20) per click-out, below compari's price levels (not verified) and below Google Shopping.

**Sequencing that solves chicken-and-egg (inference):**
1. List every shop for free from public pages and feeds, so users get complete coverage.
2. Monetize through 2Performant and Profitshare from day one, with no sales team.
3. Give merchants a free dashboard: clicks sent, their price rank, and lowest-price alerts. This is proof of value and the hook for upselling.
4. Launch self-serve CPC through a prepaid wallet. Compari's 150 RON minimum top-up is a reference point. Sell clearly labelled "sponsored" slots that **never alter the lowest-final-price ranking** the product promises.
5. Consider a marketplace or checkout only much later. Compari and Skroutz do this with group capital and logistics.

**Traffic needed for CPC to make sense (estimate):** 50 merchants × 500 click-outs each = 25k click-outs a month. At 0.4 click-outs per session, that is about 60k sessions a month, reached around month 9 in the base case.

**Paid-ranking disclosure:** it is likely legally required in the EU (Omnibus/UCPD transparency of paid ranking). This was not verified in this research, so check it with a lawyer.

### Gaps
- Compari's actual CPC price table (the XLSX is behind Cloudflare). ShopMania, price.ro and Pricy's current models and traffic were not found. The split of compari or Heureka revenue between CPC and marketplace was not found.
- No reliable historical source was found on how Heureka, Árukereső or Skroutz sequenced free versus paid listings in their early years.

## 3. Display advertising (AdSense, managed networks, programmatic, native, newsletters)

### Takeaway
Display ads are a minor, late revenue line for Romanian-language traffic:
- Raptive needs at least 40–50% of traffic from the US, UK, Canada, Australia or New Zealand.
- Ezoic raised entry to 250k monthly users for new sites from 19 Feb 2026.
- Mediavine Journey is technically open at 1,000 sessions.

That leaves AdSense (€70 payout threshold) or direct and programmatic Romanian demand. No published Romanian web RPM benchmark was found; the only proxy is a YouTube RPM of about $1.15. Each ad that diverts a click-out worth about €0.14 can cost more than it earns, so keep ads off offer lists. Retail media (sponsored products and brand ads), as practised by Ceneo, idealo and Heureka, is the better "ads" business once traffic exists.

### Cited Findings
- **Raptive:**
  - Minimum dropped to **25,000 monthly pageviews** on 16 Oct 2025, from 100,000.
  - Sites with 25k–99,999 pageviews need 50% of traffic from the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand and/or Australia. Sites with 100k+ need 40%.
  - Also required: long-form content on most pages, a domain at least 6 months old, and Google Analytics set up.
  - Sources: [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/raptive-drops-pageview-requirement-to-25-000-monthly-visits/); [Raptive Help](https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032840891-How-do-I-apply-to-Raptive)
- **Mediavine:**
  - Journey's threshold dropped to **1,000 sessions a month** on 15 Jan 2026, from 10,000. It pays a 70% revenue share and needs the Grow plugin installed for at least 30 days. — [Jupiter](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify); [Productive Blogging](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/)
  - Sources conflict on the main network: 50k sessions a month versus about $5k a year of ad revenue. — [Jupiter](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify)
- **Ezoic:** new sites added after **19 Feb 2026 must have 250k+ monthly users**. Grandfathered sites lose status after 7+ days without the integration. All sites must be in an AdSense-supported language. An Incubator program is the fallback. — [Ezoic support](https://support.ezoic.com/kb/article/getting-started-ezoics-requirements); [Ezoic Incubator](https://www.ezoic.com/incubator)
- **AdSense:**
  - Payment threshold is **€70** or $100. The identity-verification threshold is now $0, and address verification triggers at $10. — [Google AdSense Help](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en)
  - AdSense has 42.5% share among ad platforms detected on Romanian sites in 2026. — [wmtips](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/advertising/country/ro/)
  - AdSense moved to pay-per-impression in 2024 (secondary source). — [alejandrorioja.com](https://alejandrorioja.com/ar/adsense-revenue/)
- **RPM proxy only:** YouTube RPM for Romania is about **$1.15 per 1,000 views** (rank 43 of 90 markets), vs Poland at $1.73 (data through July 2026). This is a third-party figure for YouTube, not web display. — [dynamoi RO](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/ro); [dynamoi PL](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/pl)
- **Header bidding in Romania:** Prebid was found on about 45% of 133 Romanian header-bidding sites, and Amazon's Transparent Ad Marketplace on about 18% (BuiltWith, 1 Jul 2025). — [BuiltWith](https://trends.builtwith.com/ads/header-bidding/country/Romania). Ringier Romania adopted PubMatic OpenWrap header bidding in 2018. — [PubMatic](https://pubmatic.com/news/ringier-romania-programmatic-partnership/)
- **Ads can cost affiliate approval:** Profitshare excludes affiliate sites with pop-ups or pop-unders or with more than 50% advertising content (clause 4.3). — [Profitshare Terms](https://profitshare.ro/terms)
- **Retail-media benchmarks:**
  - Ceneo's advertising revenue was PLN 22.16M, 13.4% of H1 2025 revenue. — [Allegro H1 2025](https://biznes.pap.pl/download/attachment/52459388/DOC.20250918.52459388.zal01_2025_Half-Year_Report_Of_Allegro.eu_Group.pdf)
  - idealo launched "idealo Ads" retail media in May 2026; it was not yet material to H1 revenue. — [retail-news.de, 16 Sep 2026](https://retail-news.de/idealo-umsatz-h1-2026-internationales-wachstum/)
  - Heureka created a "Heureka Ads" brand for brands in 2022. — [MediaGuru, 22 Feb 2022](https://www.mediaguru.cz/heureka-group-ma-novou-identitu-i-tri-nove-b2b-znacky)

### Inferences
- **Opportunity cost (estimate):** a display impression at a €1 RPM earns €0.001. A click-out earns about €0.08–0.14, roughly 100× more. If an ad unit diverts even 1% of click-outs from a page, it loses more than it earns. So put ads only on non-commercial pages (guides, long-tail price-history archives, the blog), never next to offer lists, and keep pages well below Profitshare's 50%-ads exclusion.
- **Modelling assumption:** display RPM for Romanian pageviews of €0.5–1.2, which is unverified. At 1M pageviews that is only €500–1,200 a month.
- **Managed networks are effectively unavailable.** Raptive and Mediavine are built for US and UK traffic, and Raptive's geography rule is explicit. Ezoic's 250k-user floor means a Romanian site would rely on AdSense, Prebid with Romanian SSP demand, or direct deals.
- **Better ad products:**
  - Newsletter or alert-email sponsorships sold directly to retailers.
  - Sponsored "deal of the day" slots, clearly labelled.
  - From months 12–24, a self-serve sponsored-products format, a retail-media-lite version of the Ceneo, idealo and Heureka path.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Romanian web display RPM benchmark (AdSense or Prebid) was found. Local Romanian ad networks and SSPs were not researched (search budget). No Romanian newsletter sponsorship rates were found. Mediavine's geography rules were not verified.

## 6. Google CSS (Comparison Shopping Service) partner program

### Takeaway
Becoming a Google CSS is feasible on paper for a Romanian comparison site (Romania is in the EEA). The core requirements are a real comparison site, at least 50 distinct merchant domains per country, and a redirect to the merchant's purchase page. Merchants value the roughly 20% Shopping-auction discount (16–21% effective). But CSS margins compete toward zero: Heureka's CEO said CSS brings "no revenue or margin" because agencies pass the discount back to e-shops. Treat CSS as a **merchant-acquisition wedge** (months 9–18), not a profit centre. White-label CSS providers allow a faster start.

### Cited Findings
- **Google's CSS requirements:**
  - The business must be registered in a CSS-program country.
  - The site must let users search and compare products, including price and selling conditions for the same product across merchants.
  - It must show products from **at least 50 distinct merchant domains per participating country**, and those merchants must ship there.
  - It must send users to a page where they can buy.
  - It cannot share a name or logo with a goods seller, its display name must match its business or domain name, and it cannot filter most queries down to fewer than 3 merchants.
  - Transactable offers are out of scope.
  - "CSS product pages" need an active CSS Center account and are limited to listed countries.
  - Source: [Google Merchant Center Help: CSS program requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7524491?hl=en)
- **Older tiered requirement:** an older third-party source describes 100 merchants for a standard partner and 500 for premium. This is likely outdated. — [Productcaster](https://www.productcaster.com/?p=3032)
- **Discount size:**
  - With a CSS partner, retailers pay 20% less margin on their auction bid. Channable frames it as "0% deducted → 25% more bidding power". — [Channable](https://www.channable.com/blog/save-money-css-partner)
  - The effective advantage is 16–18% according to Cobiro. — [Cobiro](https://cobiro.com/articles/google-shopping-css-discount)
  - About 19–21% of CPC measured, per one merchant-focused source. — [eCommerce Germany](https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/the-role-of-google-css-partners-in-germanys-e-commerce-landscape/)
- **Partner fees vary:** one Shopify app charges $25 a month per connected Merchant Center account, and some agencies bundle a CSS for free. — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/google-css-shopxyz-comparison-shopping-partner); [Channable CSS](https://channable.com/products/css)
- **White-label coverage:** white-label CSS covers the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland (vendor source). — [Cobiro whitelabel requirements](https://cobiro.com/whitelabel-css/getting-started/requirements.html)
- **Heureka's view [26 Jun 2020]:**
  - Heureka.cz sued Google for about CZK 395M of lost profit over 2013–2017 Shopping self-preferencing.
  - Its CEO Braverman said only about 1% of users click the "by Heureka" links in Shopping units.
  - Under CSS, agencies running look-alike comparison sites pass the 20% discount back to e-shops, so the margin "erodes into zero".
  - A Grant Thornton study commissioned by idealo found Shopping-ad prices averaged 13.7% above the cheapest offers.
  - Source: [Lupa.cz](https://www.lupa.cz/clanky/tomas-braverman-heureka-cz-podavame-zalobu-na-google-chceme-395-milionu-usleho-zisku/)
- **Romanian Shopping CPC:** up about 27% in 2.5 years. — [Economica (Webdigital advertorial)](https://www.economica.net/analiza-webdigital-efectele-presiunii-jucatorilor-externi-asupra-pietei-de-ecommerce-din-romania_965837.html)

### Inferences
**Path (estimate):**
1. **Months 6–12:** resell a white-label CSS to merchants already listed on Pretoscop, to learn and build relationships.
2. **Months 12–18:** once 50+ merchant domains are live on the comparison site, apply as an own CSS.

**Monetization:** a €20–50 monthly fee per Merchant Center account, or 3–5% of ad spend. Value beyond fees:
- "By Pretoscop" exposure in Shopping ads.
- Product-feed access, which reduces scraping.
- A sales channel for CPC and B2B data.

**Effort:** CSS is operationally an agency business, with feed management, campaign support and Google's policies. It distracts a two-person team, so delay it until the core site has traction.

### Gaps
- Whether Romania is on the "CSS product pages" country list. The number of CSSs targeting Romania. Google's 2025–2026 changes to CSS or Shopping units under the DMA. Whether comparison sites can use Merchant Center free listings directly (not verified).

## 7. Cashback and coupons

### Takeaway
Romanian cashback is crowded and low-margin:
- CashClub has 1,000+ stores.
- The "Bani înapoi" app offers up to 20% at 470 shops.
- Tipli and CashOut operate in the market.
- Banks fund cashback: Banca Transilvania gave 10% on online payments on Tuesdays (until end-2024), and Raiffeisen Digital Bank ran a 50% cashback promotion for Black Friday.
- OnBuy promises at least 1% instant cashback since March 2026.

Cashback means giving away much of a €3.67 average commission. It needs written network approval (Profitshare clause 4.10), it is paid only after return windows, and it is often excluded by merchants because of last-click conflicts. For Pretoscop, **coupons that lower the "final price"** fit the product better. Cashback should be a later loyalty feature at most.

### Cited Findings
- **CashClub:** 1,000+ partner stores; launched a SeedBlink crowdfunding round in April 2025. — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/cashclub-seedblink-crowdfunding-april-2025)
- **"Bani înapoi" app:** promises up to 20% at 470 online shops. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1613945355)
- **How cashback is funded:** the cashback portal returns part of the commission the shop pays the platform (Tipli, 2021). — [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/fiti-recompensati-pentru-orice-cumparatura-online-cu-tipli-4248594). Cashback is confirmed only after a waiting period that covers returns and cancellations. — [Antena3 (sponsored content)](https://www.antena3.ro/continut-platit/cum-sa-folosesti-cashback-ul-in-avantajul-tau-742305.html)
- **Bank cashback:**
  - Banca Transilvania gave 10% cashback on online payments every Tuesday until 31 Dec 2024. — [Capital.ro](https://www.capital.ro/se-returneaza-banii-decizie-pentru-clientii-banca-transilvania-si-bcr.html)
  - Raiffeisen Digital Bank ran a 50% cashback Black Friday promotion through Payrate. — [Revista Biz](https://www.revistabiz.ro/black-friday-de-la-raiffeisen-digital-bank-cu-50-cashback-pentru-clienti-prin-payrate/)
  - Overview of Romanian cashback cards: [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/Finante-Banci/288538/cum-poti-castiga-bani-in-timp-ce-faci-cumparaturi-carduri-si-ofertele-cashback-din-romania.html)
- **OnBuy entry [27 Mar 2026]:** the UK marketplace OnBuy entered Romania with at least 1% instant cashback on every purchase. The cashback can be withdrawn after 30 days. — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/un-nou-rival-emag-intra-in-romania-promite-bani-inapoi-la-fiecare-cumparatura.html)
- **Coupon aggregators exist:**
  - ReduceriPreturi.ro (undated). — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/p-reduceripreturi-platforma-coduri-de-reducere-htm-30102)
  - Mobilissimo runs a daily "CUPOANELE ZILEI" coupon roundup. — [Mobilissimo](https://www.mobilissimo.ro/stiri-despre/cupoane%20reduceri)
- **Network rules:** Profitshare bans rewarding clicks "except cashback-type websites with prior written approval" (4.10). — [Profitshare Terms](https://profitshare.ro/terms). 2Performant lets merchants choose which traffic-source types they accept. — [2Performant Annual Report 2025](https://www.bvb.ro/info/Raportari/2P/2P_20260423225924_Raport-anual-2025-RO.pdf)
- **Chrome Web Store affiliate policy [11 Mar 2025]:**
  - Affiliate links, codes or cookies are allowed only when they give a direct, transparent user benefit tied to the extension's core function.
  - The affiliate program must be disclosed on the listing, in the UI and before install.
  - Each affiliate action needs a user action and must deliver a benefit at that moment.
  - Background injection with no user action, or with no discount, cashback or donation, is banned.
  - Sources: [Chrome for Developers blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-policy-update-affiliate-ads-2025); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/03/11/google-chrome-affiliate-extension-policy-honey/)

### Inferences
**Economics (estimate):** sharing 50% of the €3.67 average affiliate commission gives the user about €1.8 per order, about 3% of a €60 order. That does not stand out against 10% bank cashback promotions. It also halves Pretoscop's revenue per sale.

**Better uses of coupons:**
- Include network coupon codes in the "final price" calculation and badge them. This is a direct user benefit and also permitted for extensions.
- Never auto-inject affiliate cookies.

**Conflict risk:** coupon and cashback clicks happen at checkout and overwrite earlier last-click credit. Retailers dislike this, and it may be why some restrict such traffic. Pretoscop should position itself as a price-transparency or content site, not a cashback or coupon site, when applying to networks.

### Gaps
- Picodi and Cashback World (myWorld) metrics in Romania. The revenue-share splits used by Romanian cashback sites. Romanian coupon-site traffic and revenue. Whether 2Performant has published rules on cashback.

## 8. Other revenue streams (marketplace commission, lead-gen, cross-border affiliates, Black Friday events, white-label and API)

### Takeaway
Several adjacent lines exist, but most are either capital-heavy (a marketplace like compari's at 3–5.5%), crowded or state-owned (telecom and car-insurance comparators), or carry brand risk (Temu affiliate pays up to 30% but would undermine a "fake-discount watchdog" positioning).

The highest-value "event" is Black Friday:
- eMAG took 986M lei in orders in one day in 2025.
- 2Performant's BF-window sales rose 13.3%.
- Q4 was 28.8% of 2Performant's 2025 turnover.

## 9. Growth and distribution playbook (SEO, AI search, browser extension, bots/community, PR)

### Takeaway
SEO remains the main acquisition channel. Google's documentation makes clear what a comparison site gets:
- **Product snippet** rich results (with AggregateOffer lowPrice and offerCount) on single-product pages.
- **Not** merchant listings, which require on-page purchase.
- It must avoid scaled-content, thin-affiliate and doorway patterns. Every indexable page needs unique value: price history, the 30-day lowest price, final price including shipping, and offer count.

AI search is a live threat. Google's AI Mode launched in Romanian in October 2025, and Czech press reports that Google and AI are "crushing" Heureka. Mitigate with owned channels: alerts and email, a browser extension, Telegram and push. Lean into PR around Romania's 30-day reference-price rule and ANPC enforcement, the strongest earned-media hook.

### Cited Findings
**Structured data and spam policies**
- **Product snippets (page updated 2026-09-08):**
  - They cover "other product related pages such as product reviews and aggregator sites".
  - Required: `name` plus one of `review`, `aggregateRating` or `offers`.
  - `AggregateOffer` "can be used for a product that is being sold by multiple merchants". Required: `lowPrice` and `priceCurrency`. Recommended: `highPrice` and `offerCount`.
  - Product rich results "only support pages that focus on a single product". The price-drop enhancement needs `Offer`, not `AggregateOffer`.
  - Source: [Google Search Central: Product snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet)
- **Merchant listings:** "Only pages where a shopper can purchase a product are eligible … not pages with links to other sites that sell the product." — [Google Search Central: Merchant listing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing)
- **Spam policies (updated 2026-08-28)** — [Google Search Central: Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies):
  - Scaled content abuse: many pages generated mainly to manipulate rankings.
  - Site reputation: third-party content placed on a host to exploit its ranking signals. **Within the EEA, such pages may be categorized separately from the main domain** rather than getting a manual action.
  - Thin affiliation: affiliate pages with copied merchant descriptions are thin, while "original product reviews, rigorous testing and ratings" add value.

## 11. Traffic-to-revenue model, months 1–24 (formulas, sourced parameters, scenarios)

### Takeaway
At realistic Romanian parameters, Pretoscop earns about **€9–49 per 1,000 sessions** (base case about €26), mostly from affiliate click-outs.
- **Base case** (400k sessions a month by month 24): about €3.9k a month at month 12 and about €15.6k a month at month 24, or about €15–18k in year 1 and about €118–132k in year 2.
- **Conservative case:** under €1.5k a month at month 24.

Reaching about €5k a month needs roughly 190k sessions a month in the base case. The biggest swing factors are click-outs per session, eMAG monetization, attribution leakage, and SEO growth.

### Inferences (model; every non-cited parameter is an **assumption**)

**Formulas**
- Affiliate revenue = S × k × m_aff × EPC_eff
  - S = sessions per month.
  - k = click-outs per session (assumption: 0.25 conservative / 0.40 base / 0.50 optimistic; no public benchmark found).
  - m_aff = share of click-outs landing on merchants with an approved affiliate program (assumption: 0.40 / 0.55 / 0.70). It is low if eMAG stays unmonetized and higher if Profitshare approves.
  - EPC_eff = €0.137 network EPC × attribution factor (assumption: about 0.6 / 0.8 / 0.95), giving €0.08 / €0.11 / €0.13.
- Equivalent form: EPC = CR × AOV × commission. Electronics example: 2.20% × €190.77 × 3.2% ≈ €0.134.
- CPC revenue = S × k × m_cpc × CPC.
  - m_cpc = share of click-outs going to paying CPC merchants, starting at month 12 or 18 (assumption).
  - CPC = €0.05 / €0.08 / €0.10 (inference from merchant break-even, section 2).
  - In the script, CPC clicks cannibalize half their share from affiliate clicks.
- Display revenue = S × pageviews per session (2.0 / 2.5 / 3.0) × RPM (€0.5 / €0.8 / €1.2) ÷ 1000. All are assumptions.
- Subscriptions = MAU (about 0.6 × S) × paid conversion (0 / 0.3% / 0.5%) × €3 per month, from month 12 (speculative).
- B2B = number of clients × monthly revenue per client (e.g., base: 3 × €200 at month 18 and 8 × €250 at month 24).

## 12. Key business risks and mitigations

### Takeaway
The dominant risks are:
- Dependence on Google and AI search (the Heureka and Kelkoo precedents).
- eMAG's weight combined with Profitshare's discretionary approval, with eMAG now loss-making and running its own "Super Preț" comparison.
- Incumbents with capital: compari's marketplace at 3–5.5% and Skroutz/Blackstone already present in Romania.
- Thin per-click economics: €0.08–0.14 EPC and low Romanian ad RPM.
- Seasonality and commission reversals.

Mitigations: owned channels, B2B data revenue, diversification across networks, strict neutrality and legal hygiene in fake-discount claims, and Black Friday-centred PR.

### Inferences (risk → mitigation)

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Google algorithm, AI Mode or AI Overviews cut organic clicks | High | Owned channels (alerts, extension, Telegram, newsletter); citable data studies; brand building through PR; structured data; diversify into B2B revenue that is independent of search |
| eMAG unmonetized or Profitshare refusal | High | Get written approval early. Keep eMAG listed regardless, for trust. Monetize the long tail (822 productive 2Performant shops) and eMAG marketplace sellers' own shops. Sell CPC to challengers. Apply via Profitshare for other programs |
| Retailers block crawling (bot protection) | Medium–High | Prefer feeds and APIs (2Performant and Profitshare product feeds, CSS feeds); polite crawling; merchant opt-in dashboard; legal review of terms and database rights. (Observation: compari.ro served a Cloudflare challenge to automated fetches during this research) |
| Compari incumbency or Skroutz expansion | Medium | Differentiate on verified price history and fake-discount detection, shipping-inclusive final price, and speed and UX; target niches (electronics, appliances, baby) first |
| Low Romanian ad RPM; ads hurt EPC | Medium | Keep display minimal; prefer sponsored and retail-media placements sold directly |
| Commission reversals, attribution loss, app hand-off | Medium | Deep-link with network parameters; prefer networks with first-party attribution (2Performant "Big Bear"); track approval rates per merchant; drop merchants with low approval |
| Seasonality (Q4 ≈ 29%) | Medium | Budget cash on a 12-month basis; B2B monthly recurring revenue smooths the seasons; pre-sell Black Friday sponsored slots |
| Defamation or unfair-competition claims from "fake discount" flags | Medium | Publish a transparent methodology (30-day lowest price, as in law); show the evidence (price chart and timestamps); allow a right of reply; avoid naming retailers in a sensationalist way; consult a lawyer before PR |
| Chrome Web Store or network policy violations | Low–Medium | Affiliate only on user click with disclosure; no background injection; declare all media to the networks |
| Founder bandwidth (2 people) | High | Sequence: affiliate + SEO + alerts (months 0–6) → extension + B2B dashboard (6–12) → CPC + CSS (12–18) → retail media and data licensing (18–24) |
