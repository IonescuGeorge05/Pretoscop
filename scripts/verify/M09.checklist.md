M09 — 5-minute founder check (admin)
1. `make dev`, open http://localhost:3000/admin, enter your email (it must be in ADMIN_EMAILS in .env),
   open the magic link from Mailpit (http://localhost:8025). You land on the dashboard.
2. Sources (/admin/surse): pause a source with its kill switch; the dashboard shows it as paused;
   turn it back on. "Run now" queues a run (all gates still apply in the worker).
3. Potriviri (/admin/potriviri): clear the review items with the keyboard (j/k, 1–5 accept,
   n new product, r reject, b bundle, a accessory, / search). Then judge the 10 random automatic
   matches (Corect / Greșit): "Greșit" unlinks the offer for good.
4. Produse (/admin/produse): search by title, id, GTIN or MPN; on a product edit the title or
   category, add a GTIN (the check digit is validated), merge a duplicate into it and undo the
   merge; split offers into a new product; hide and unhide.
5. Oferte (/admin/oferte): unlink or blacklist an offer; list the blacklist.
6. Audit (/admin/audit): every action above has a row with you as the actor.
