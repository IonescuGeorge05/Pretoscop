M19 — 5-minute founder check (accounts and price alerts)
0. Alerts are off by default: turn on `alerts.email.enabled` in /admin/flaguri (until then the product
   page shows "În curând"). Mail goes to Mailpit locally (http://localhost:8025).
1. Open a product page with a current offer (or the throwaway fixture:
   `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/db e2e:fixture alerts-create` prints its slug and id; remove it later with
   `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/db e2e:fixture alerts-drop off`). In "Alertă de preț": a threshold under
   the current total, your email, tick the consent box (never pre-ticked) → "Verifică emailul".
2. Mailpit: "Confirmă alerta de preț…" names the product, the condition and the exact consent text
   with its version (alertConsent.v1). Opening the link changes nothing until you press "Confirm alerta".
3. `make psql` →
   `select status, consent_at, consent_ip_hash, consent_text_version, confirmed_at, confirm_method from alerts order by id desc limit 1;`
   (daily-salted hashes, never a raw IP).
4. `pnpm alerts:simulate <productId> 10` → Mailpit: "[SIMULARE] Preț mai mic…" with the total, shop,
   delivery, "Verificat de Pretoscop pe …", "Vezi oferta" (/out/…?p=alert) and a stop link; Mailpit
   → Headers shows List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post. Nothing is written to price history.
5. Click "Oprește această alertă" → one button → done. Then
   `select withdrawn_at, withdraw_method from consent_records order by id desc limit 1;` (evidence kept 3 years).
6. /alerte: log in with the emailed link; edit or delete an alert, "Descarcă datele mele" (JSON),
   "Șterge contul". /admin/alerte shows volumes and the data-request lookup.
7. `make verify M=19` prints OK (the e2e covers dedupe, unconfirmed alerts, one-click unsubscribe).
