M27 — 5-minute founder check (Telegram bot and web push)
Everything is off by default: the flags `alerts.push.enabled`, `alerts.telegram.enabled` and
`telegram.deals.enabled` (/admin/flaguri), and nothing runs without the keys in `.env` (setup D2).
`make verify M=27` already ran every flow against local stand-ins (a mock Bot API, a mock push
service, test VAPID keys). The steps below use the real services once D2 is done.

0. Setup (D2, once): create the bot with @BotFather and put `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` and
   `TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME` (without @) in `.env`; run `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/notify vapid:keys`
   and put both lines in `.env` (keep them in both password managers). Restart the web and the
   worker. Turn on `alerts.email.enabled`, `alerts.push.enabled` and `alerts.telegram.enabled`.
1. Web push: log in on /alerte (magic link). In "Canale de notificare → Notificări în browser",
   the consent box is unticked and the button stays grey until you tick it; tick it, press
   "Activează notificările pe acest dispozitiv", allow notifications in the browser's prompt. Your
   browser appears in the list ("Chrome · Windows", …). Press "Trimite o notificare de test": a
   "Pretoscop: notificare de test" notification appears; clicking it opens /alerte.
   On an iPhone (Safari, not the Home Screen app) the section explains "Adaugă pe ecranul principal".
2. Telegram: in "Telegram", tick the consent box, press "Conectează Telegram", then "Deschide
   Telegram" and Start. The bot answers "Gata, contul tău Pretoscop este conectat" (and "Dacă nu tu
   ai cerut conectarea, trimite /oprire"). Reload /alerte: "Conectat din …". The link is gone
   from the page and from the address bar; pressing Start on the same link again is refused.
3. In Telegram: `/urmareste <link of a product page> <a price under today's total>` → "Urmăresc …";
   `/alertele` lists it with a stop button. On /alerte the alert shows "· pe Telegram".
4. Simulated drop: `pnpm alerts:simulate <productId> 10 --email <your address>` → a "[SIMULARE] Preț
   mai mic" message in Telegram with total, shop, delivery, the check time and "Vezi oferta" /
   "Oprește alerta" buttons (the simulation ignores quiet hours; real alerts never go 22:00–08:00).
   Move another alert to "În browser (notificări)" in "Modifică" and simulate again: a browser
   notification with the same facts.
5. `make psql` →
   `select purpose, text_version, confirm_method, withdrawn_at from consent_records order by id desc limit 5;`
   shows `alert_push`/`pushConsent.v1` and `alert_telegram`/`telegramConsent.v1` next to the email
   consent; `select chat_id, status from telegram_links;` holds only the chat number (no name, no
   phone); `select device_label from push_subscriptions;` holds a label, never the User-Agent.
6. Withdraw: `/oprire` in Telegram (the alerts on Telegram pause; /alerte offers "Conectează" again);
   "Elimină" next to your browser. "Descarcă datele mele" (JSON) lists `pushSubscriptions` and
   `telegram`; "Șterge contul" removes both.
7. Turn the flags off again: /alerte says "pornesc în curând", the bot stops reading Telegram within
   a minute, nothing is sent. Optional: create the public deals channel, add the bot as admin, set
   `TELEGRAM_DEALS_CHAT_ID` and `telegram.deals.enabled`; the 09:15 post lists the day's verified drops
   with the same rules as /reduceri (≥ 14 days of history), or nothing on a day without them.
