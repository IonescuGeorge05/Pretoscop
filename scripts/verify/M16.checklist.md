M16 — 5-minute founder check (SEO: robots, sitemaps, structured data, 404s, speed)
1. The prototype is closed to search engines: open /robots.txt on the dev server (`make dev`, then
   http://localhost:3000/robots.txt): "User-agent: *" / "Disallow: /". Every page carries
   `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` while SITE_INDEXABLE=false (view the source).
   At launch, SITE_INDEXABLE=true opens it: `make verify M=16` checks that version too (AI crawlers
   such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and CCBot stay refused, as the Open Icecat licence requires; /out, /admin
   and /api stay closed; search results stay noindex; a sitemap line appears).
2. Open /sitemap.xml: an index of pages.xml (home, categories, shops, trust and legal pages, approved
   reports) and the files the worker writes nightly (products, categories, brands, shops). Only
   product pages that may be indexed are listed (two live offers, or 14 days of history, and not
   provisional). Rebuild now: `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/worker sitemap:build`.
3. Rich results: copy the HTML of a product page with two or more shops (view source) into Google's
   Rich Results Test (https://search.google.com/test/rich-results, "Code" tab) for 3 products:
   Product with AggregateOffer (lowest and highest price, number of offers) and up to 10 offers
   with the seller, plus the breadcrumb. The home page has Organization and WebSite + search box.
4. A wrong address shows a real page: /p/ceva-999999999, /c/tv-audio/nimic and /magazin/nimic answer
   404 with "Pagina nu există" (or "Produsul nu mai este disponibil"), also with JavaScript turned off.
   Check on a production build (`pnpm --filter @pretoscop/web build && pnpm --filter @pretoscop/web start`):
   under `make dev` these pages show the same text but answer 200.
5. Share a product link in a chat app: the Pretoscop image and the product title appear.
6. Speed on a phone: `make verify M=16` prints "M16 budget: LCP … ms; JavaScript … kB". LCP must stay
   under 2,5 s. JavaScript: the shared site shell is about 150 kB compressed (React + Next.js), so the
   docs/03 target "under 150 kB on product pages" is not met (about 158 kB); the product page itself
   adds under 20 kB (the price chart loads when you scroll to it). Decide whether that is acceptable.
7. Before launch (B6): add the site to Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
8. `make verify M=16` prints OK.
