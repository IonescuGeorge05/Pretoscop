M26 — 5-minute founder check (browser extension)
The lookup API answers only while the flag `extension.lookup.enabled` is on (/admin/flaguri; off by
default). `make verify M=26` already ran the extension in Chromium against local copies of shop
pages. The steps below use your own browser on the real eMAG site: that is you browsing, not our
crawler; the extension sends nothing but the page's product identifiers to your local server.

0. `make dev` (web on http://localhost:3000, dev database). Turn on `extension.lookup.enabled` in
   /admin/flaguri. Build a copy pointed at it:
   `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/extension build --target=chrome --out=dist/local --api=http://localhost:3000 --site=http://localhost:3000`
1. Chrome → `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → "Load unpacked" → `apps/extension/dist/local`.
   The details page lists no permissions; site access is limited to the five shops.
2. Pick a tracked eMAG product: `make psql` →
   `select o.url from offers o join shops s on s.id = o.shop_id where s.slug = 'emag' and o.product_id is not null and o.active order by o.last_checked_at desc limit 3;`
   Open one of the addresses in Chrome. Within a second or two a "Pretoscop · istoricul prețului"
   panel appears bottom right: the cheapest total now and where ("la eMAG …" or another shop),
   "Istoric insuficient" while the product has fewer than 14 observed days (otherwise "Preț bun",
   "Preț obișnuit" or "Peste obișnuit"), the sparkline, "Minim 30 de zile", prices written
   `1.234,56 lei`. "Vezi toate ofertele și istoricul pe Pretoscop" opens http://localhost:3000/p/… in
   a new tab, with nothing after the slug.
3. No link changed: on the eMAG page, open DevTools → Console and run
   `[...document.links].filter(a => !a.href.includes('emag.ro')).map(a => a.href)` with the panel
   showing and after closing it — the same short list both times (eMAG's own off-site links, if
   any; never a Pretoscop or affiliate link: the panel's link lives inside its shadow root). In
   Elements, the only new node is `<div id="pretoscop-ext-host">` at the end of `<html>`.
4. Keyboard: click inside the panel, press Escape: it closes (and stays closed until you reload).
   Tab reaches the × button and the link.
5. An eMAG category page (e.g. https://www.emag.ro/telefoane-mobile/c) shows no panel; neither does
   a product we do not track. DevTools → Network on the extension's service worker
   (`chrome://extensions` → "service worker") shows one `api/ext/lookup` request per product page
   and none for the category page.
6. Turn `extension.lookup.enabled` off, open another tracked eMAG product: no panel (the API says
   `disabled`). Turn it back on only if you keep testing.
7. Optional: Firefox 140+ → `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` → "Load Temporary Add-on…" →
   build with `--target=firefox --out=dist/local-ff …` and pick its `manifest.json`; Firefox lists
   the declared data collection (browsing activity, website content); repeat step 2.
8. Release gate (not met yet, so do not publish): `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/db ext:resolution emag altex`
   prints the share of our eMAG offers the lookup resolves (≥ 70 % needed, for Altex too) and how
   many have offers at two or more shops.
