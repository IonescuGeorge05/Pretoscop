# Scraping feasibility recon — top Romanian online retailers (hands-on probes 2026-10-27 + tooling/proxy/cost research, as of Oct 2026)

Method & legend (read first):
- **O = observation** from my own polite probes on **2026-10-27 (14:49–15:04 UTC)**; **I = inference**. Everything not marked O is either cited web research or an inference.
- Plain probe = `curl 8.7.1` (macOS, HTTP/2, `--compressed`) with UA `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; PretoscopResearch/0.1; +mailto:rotariu.daniel.paul@gmail.com)`. Browser-like probe = `curl_cffi 0.17.0` `impersonate="chrome"` (Chrome TLS/HTTP2 fingerprint + Chrome UA + `Accept-Language: ro-RO`). Max 6 requests/domain, ≥3.6 s + random 0.2–1.5 s between requests to the same domain, stop on 403/429/challenge. 115 requests in total across 40 domains (max 5 per domain). Full log in the last section.
- **Vantage point caveat (O):** the probe machine's egress IP geolocated to **London, GB, ASN AS5631** (looked up once via ipinfo.io; IP not recorded). Results from a Romanian residential line or a Hetzner/OVH datacenter may differ — several blocks (CEL timeout, evoMAG CAPTCHA, Akamai resets) could be IP-reputation/geo driven.
- Extra evidence without touching retailers: Common Crawl CDX index (CC-MAIN-2026-39 = Sept 2026; CC-MAIN-2026-34 = Aug 2026) status codes that **CCBot** (an honest, identified crawler) received on each shop host. Caveat: Cloudflare classifies CCBot as an AI crawler, so CCBot 403s on Cloudflare shops may reflect "block AI bots" settings rather than generic bot blocking (I).
- WebSearch quota for the whole session was exhausted mid-task (shared 200-search cap); remaining research used direct fetches of known primary URLs only. Unverified items are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Per-retailer feasibility: robots, sitemaps, rendering, structured data, EAN, Omnibus, marketplace, APIs, anti-bot, plain vs browser-like, page weight

### Takeaway
Of 40 domains, **17 shops were reachable by our honest bot**. In **12** of them, prices were extracted from server HTML/JSON: eMAG, IT Galaxy, forit, Libris, Elefant, Bonami, JYSK, Dedeman, Carrefour, Auchan, Flip, Publi24. In **5** the site was reachable, but either the price was not in the raw HTML or no product URL could be found: EuroGSM, Petmart, Vivre, About You, Mega Image.

**22 domains blocked, challenged or were unreachable** on robots.txt, sitemap or the homepage:
- Most electronics specialists: Altex, Media Galaxy, Flanco, PC Garage, evoMAG, CEL, Vexio, iStyle, plus QuickMobile (in maintenance).
- Notino, Douglas, Sephora, Answear, Fashion Days, Cărturești, Leroy Merlin (DataDome), Hornbach, Decathlon, Dr.Max, Noriel, OLX, and Hervis (which now redirects to SportsDirect).

nod.ro is a corporate WordPress site, not a shop.

Crucially, Common Crawl shows **Akamai/EdgeOne-protected shops (Altex, Media Galaxy, Douglas, Sephora, Fashion Days, SportsDirect) serve the *known* bot CCBot with mostly 200s** — so access is gated on bot reputation/verification, not on robots.txt, which argues for verified-bot status and retailer allowlisting rather than evasion.

### Inferences
- Electronics/IT is the hardest vertical: of 13 consumer electronics shops probed, only eMAG, IT Galaxy, forit and EuroGSM were open to an honest bot; Altex/Media Galaxy (Akamai), Flanco/PC Garage/Vexio/iStyle (Cloudflare), evoMAG (custom CAPTCHA), CEL (network drop) and QuickMobile (maintenance) were not. Price comparison for electronics therefore cannot rely on polite crawling alone — feeds/partnerships (2Performant/Profitshare-type affiliate feeds, direct merchant feeds) are a prerequisite for those shops.
- Akamai shops serving CCBot (Altex 967/1,209 = 80% 200s; Douglas 90%; Sephora 87%) while resetting/403-ing our unknown bot suggests Akamai Bot Manager "known bot" allowlisting is in force; a Pretoscop bot would likely need to be added by each retailer (custom bot definition/allowlist by IP or header) — i.e., a business-development task, not an engineering one.
- Cloudflare shops 403-ing CCBot (Vexio, Notino, Dr.Max, PC Garage, Elefant, forit, Carrefour) is consistent with Cloudflare's AI-crawler blocking; yet Elefant, forit and Carrefour served *our* honest UA. So on Cloudflare the decisive factor appears to be the zone's bot-score/managed-challenge configuration plus path (Flanco: robots.txt open, sitemap challenged).
- HTTP/2 stream resets (Altex, Media Galaxy, SportsDirect) are a "silent deny" pattern; a crawler must classify `curl 92`/`RST_STREAM INTERNAL_ERROR` as a block (my first script didn't — see appendix) and back off per domain.
- JSON-LD Product is the single most valuable extractor target. It carries an offer price on 10 of the 14 product pages reached: eMAG, IT Galaxy, forit, Flip, Libris, Bonami, JYSK, Carrefour, Auchan, Publi24. Mega Image's JSON-LD (plain client) has no price. EAN/GTIN appears in JSON-LD only at Libris and Bonami; elsewhere it hides in Flixmedia attributes (IT Galaxy, Carrefour), spec tables (Elefant), platform state/API (Auchan). Matching across shops will need MPN/brand+title matching, not EAN alone.
- Omnibus text exists mostly as tooltips/i18n strings and is only populated on discounted items; the sampled products were mostly not discounted, so "not found" ≠ "not implemented". eMAG's machine-readable `lowest_price_30_days.amount` is the best structured Omnibus source observed.
- Publi24's JSON-LD exposes private individuals' names as sellers → do not store seller names for C2C classifieds (GDPR data minimisation).

### Gaps
- No browser-like (curl_cffi) comparison on the 20 blocked domains (stop rule) — whether Chrome-TLS impersonation alone passes Akamai/Cloudflare/DataDome/EdgeOne/Fastly on these specific shops is unknown.
- Probes came from a UK IP (AS5631). Romanian residential and Hetzner/OVH datacenter behaviour was not tested; CEL.ro's timeout may be geo-filtering.
- Product URL not discovered (so product page not tested) for EuroGSM, Petmart, Vivre; Altex/Media Galaxy/Flanco/PC Garage etc. content fields unknown (blocked). Child sitemaps (per-URL lastmod, URLs per file) were not fetched for any site (budget), so product counts are bounds only.
- Whether eMAG's other-seller offers ("Alte oferte") are in the product HTML: only 1 "Alte oferte" string seen; the embedded offers observed were variants. Needs a product with multiple sellers.

## 3. Discovery strategies, change detection, crawl budgets and realistic cadence

### Takeaway
Use **sitemaps for discovery and delisting**, **category listing pages for daily price refresh** (one request returns 24–71 prices), and product pages only for new items/details. At 1 request per 3–5 s per domain a single polite stream does 17,280–28,800 requests/day: enough for ~1–1.7M listing-based price observations/day per domain (at 60 items/page), but only 17–29k product pages/day — so daily product-page refresh of a 500k-offer shop is impossible politely (17–29 days per pass).

### Cited Findings
- Product sitemaps exist at most open shops (O): eMAG `offers-available-index` with 841 child files plus separate `offers-latest-index` (new) and `offers-unavailable-index` (out of stock/delisted) — [eMAG robots](https://www.emag.ro/robots.txt), [index](https://www.emag.ro/sitemaps/offers-available-index.xml); Auchan 64 `product-N.xml` — [index](https://www.auchan.ro/sitemap.xml); Dedeman 25 product sitemaps — [index](https://www.dedeman.ro/media/sitemap/sitemap.xml); Elefant 23 — [index](https://www.elefant.ro/sitemap-sitemapRO); Vivre 20 product `.xml.gz` + `vendors.xml.gz` — [index](https://ro.vivre.eu/sitemaps/index/sitemap.xml); About You 8 product `.txt` — [index](https://storage.googleapis.com/ay-mit-mct-atmo-p_about_you_sitemaps/ro/sitemap-index.xml); Leroy Merlin 8 — [index](https://www.leroymerlin.ro/sitemap-index.xml); IT Galaxy `products-0..3` + `products-latest-0` — [index](https://www.itgalaxy.ro/data/sitemaps/index.xml); Libris 426 files — [index](https://www.libris.ro/sitemap-new-url-index.xml?nofilename=on); Flip single file with 6,869 product URLs — [sitemap](https://flip.ro/sitemap.xml); Decathlon `decathlon-products-index.xml` + `marketplace-products-index.xml` — [robots](https://www.decathlon.ro/robots.txt).

### Inferences
- Recommended cadence: hot products (top-N by search/clicks, items with active promotions, Black Friday window) refreshed **daily or several times/day via listing pages**; long tail **weekly** via listing pages; product pages only on first discovery, when listing price ≠ stored price, or every 30 days for detail/EAN refresh. Delisting = absent from listing + present in eMAG `offers-unavailable`/Bonami `product_dead` sitemaps + product page 404/410.
- Index-level lastmod equal to "today" on every child file is regeneration time, not change time; it cannot drive incremental crawls. Per-URL lastmod (in child files) must be validated per site before trusting it.
- Respect parameter disallows: sort/filter URLs are off-limits on many sites, so plan category-tree traversal via plain pagination (where allowed) and sitemaps; where pagination is disallowed (About You pages >3, Noriel all `?`), use sitemaps + product pages or feeds.
- Per-domain politeness queues: one queue per registrable domain (merge `www.`/apex/CDN subdomains), token-bucket ≤1 req/3 s, global concurrency across domains, exponential backoff on 429/503/511/stream-reset with a circuit breaker (stop domain for hours after a challenge), robots.txt cached ≤24 h (RFC 9309) and re-checked before each batch.

### Gaps
- URLs per child sitemap and per-URL lastmod accuracy unknown (not fetched). Product counts for Altex/Flanco/PC Garage unknown.
- Whether listing pages at blocked shops render prices server-side (teammate said Altex listing is client-side) could not be re-checked.

## 6. Legal/ethical scraping etiquette used by well-known crawlers; robots.txt examples aimed at bots

### Takeaway
The workable etiquette standard in 2026 is: identify the bot (stable UA token + info URL), verify identity (published IPs with reverse DNS or Cloudflare Web Bot Auth signatures), obey robots.txt per RFC 9309 (cache ≤24 h, 5xx ⇒ full disallow), keep low per-domain rates with backoff, never solve challenges, and ask retailers for allowlisting/feeds. Romanian retailers' robots.txt files target SEO and AI bots and filter/price-sort URLs. None of the ~25 readable files has a User-agent group for a price-comparison bot: a grep of the probed files for price/compari/shopmania/idealo/pricerunner/pricy/storebot/ceneo/heureka/arukereso/kelkoo found no UA lines. Price-related rules only block price-filter URLs, such as `*/filtre/price/*`, `*/pret*`, `/*/pret-*-pana-la-*/*`, `?price_max=`/`?minPrice=` and `/*priceRange`.

### Cited Findings
- RFC 9309: 4xx robots.txt ⇒ "the crawler MAY access any resources"; 5xx ⇒ "the crawler MUST assume complete disallow"; cache "SHOULD NOT [be used] for more than 24 hours"; parsing limit "MUST be at least 500 kibibytes"; product tokens matched case-insensitively; crawl-delay is not part of the standard — [RFC 9309](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html).
- Cloudflare Verified Bots: bot must declare identity "deterministically, through a cryptographic Web Bot Auth signature" (or published IPs + stable UA, or reverse DNS), obey robots.txt, "maintain reasonable request rates"; categories include "Data Collection" (covers price scraping) and legacy "Aggregator"; apply via the BotBase submission form in the Cloudflare dashboard — [Cloudflare policy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/concepts/bot/verified-bots/policy/).
- Web Bot Auth: Ed25519 key, JWKS served at `/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory`, requests signed with `Signature-Input` (`tag="web-bot-auth"`, `keyid`, `created`, `expires`), `Signature`, and `Signature-Agent` headers; "A minute is often sufficient" for expiry — [Cloudflare docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/reference/bot-verification/web-bot-auth/).
- CCBot practice: UA "CCBot/2.0 (https://commoncrawl.org/faq/)", dedicated ranges with reverse DNS, published JSON ranges, warning about impostors — [Common Crawl](https://commoncrawl.org/ccbot).
- Robots.txt examples observed (O, 2026-10-27): Elefant `User-agent: spider` and `User-agent: crawler` → `Disallow: /` — [robots](https://www.elefant.ro/robots.txt); Flanco Cloudflare `Content-Signal: search=yes,ai-train=no` + full Disallow for CCBot/GPTBot/ClaudeBot etc. — [robots](https://www.flanco.ro/robots.txt); Vivre full Disallow for ~22 AI/SEO bots — [robots](https://ro.vivre.eu/robots.txt); Publi24 explicit `Allow: /` for GPTBot, CCBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot etc. — [robots](https://www.publi24.ro/robots.txt); eMAG full Disallow for 008, Yandex, Baiduspider, AhrefsBot, Synthesio — [robots](https://www.emag.ro/robots.txt); Libris for AhrefsBot, SemrushBot, DotBot, MJ12bot — [robots](https://www.libris.ro/robots.txt); price/sort-filter disallows: Elefant `*/pret*`, `*/discount*`; Flanco `*/filtre/price/*`; About You `?price_max=`, `?minPrice=`; Bonami `/*priceRange`; Carrefour `/*order=*` — links above and [Bonami](https://www.bonami.ro/robots.txt), [Carrefour](https://carrefour.ro/robots.txt), [About You](https://www.aboutyou.ro/robots.txt).
- Omnibus disclosures quoted on retailer pages (O): eMAG, Flip, Bonami, About You texts in section 1c.

### Inferences
- Pretoscop bot spec: UA `PretoscopBot/1.0 (+https://pretoscop.ro/bot)` — product token without the words "spider"/"crawler" (Elefant blocks those groups), info page listing purpose, contact, IP ranges (JSON), opt-out instructions; reverse DNS on crawler IPs (`crawl-N.pretoscop.ro`); Web Bot Auth signing from day one; apply to Cloudflare Verified Bots under "Data Collection/Aggregator"; ask Akamai-fronted retailers (Altex, Media Galaxy, Mega Image, Douglas, Sephora, Answear) to add a custom bot rule. Treat 403/429/503/511, `cf-mitigated: challenge`, HTTP/2 resets, CAPTCHA redirects and HTTP-200 interstitials as "stop and back off for ≥24 h".
- Cache aggressively (conditional requests with ETag/Last-Modified where offered), fetch listing pages instead of product pages, and avoid sort/filter/price URLs that robots files disallow.
- Database rights: EU sui generis database protection (Directive 96/9/EC) and the CJEU's 2021 *CV-Online Latvia v Melons* (C-762/19) ruling on specialised search engines indexing databases are directly relevant to systematic re-use of a retailer's catalogue; this needs legal review (I could not fetch the judgment text in this session — see Gaps).

- Production crawler lessons from this run:
