1. `make verify M=23` generates `data/reports/phase0-check/` from the live data. Open `report.md`
   and the PNGs in `charts/`: with only a few days recorded, the report says that history is
   insufficient — that honesty is the point.
2. Read `docs/press-kit/review-checklist.md` (how a report gets approved) and the Romanian
   `docs/press-kit/README.md` and `comunicat-template.md`.
3. Open http://localhost:3000/rapoarte and /rapoarte/black-friday-2026 (placeholder until approved)
   and /metodologie#rapoarte (the rules).
4. After Black Friday (eMAG and others usually run it mid to late November): run the generator for
   the BF days, review with the checklist, spot-check 5 findings, then `report:approve`.
