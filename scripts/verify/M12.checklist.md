M12 — 5-minute founder check
1. `make dev`, then open http://localhost:3000 on your phone (same Wi-Fi: http://<your-mac-ip>:3000) or with the browser's mobile view: header with search, Romanian text with ș/ț, footer links.
2. The bottom banner offers "Refuz" and "Accept" with equal weight. Open the browser's Network tab, reload: nothing is requested from another domain. Click "Accept": Plausible loads only if `NEXT_PUBLIC_PLAUSIBLE_*` is configured (it is not yet: M17 adds the self-hosted container).
3. Click "Refuz", reload: the banner stays closed and nothing third-party loads.
4. Visit http://localhost:3000/nu-exista — Romanian 404 page.
5. Optional: `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/web e2e` after `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/web build`.
