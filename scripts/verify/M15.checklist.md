M15 — 5-minute founder check (deals and discount badges)
1. Open /reduceri. Until our history covers at least 14 days the page says so, with the date the first
   verified drops can appear; it never shows a shop's own "-40%". (`pnpm --filter @pretoscop/worker
   deals:show` prints the same list in the terminal.)
2. Once drops appear: pick 3, open their product pages and check on the chart that the price really fell
   below the lowest price of the 30 days before the drop. The line under the badge names the reference
   (lei and dates) and the first day we observed the lower price; "Cum verificăm" leads to
   /metodologie#reduceri. A product whose price went back up part of the way after a lower price
   shows no badge (we observed the lower price ourselves).
3. Try the filters (7 zile / Azi, 3–30%, Doar minime istorice) and the category tabs; with JavaScript
   switched off the filters still work.
4. On a product with less than 14 days of history there is no "−X%" and no "Minim istoric".
5. Read the labels once more for tone: factual, with numbers and dates, never "fals" or "înșelătorie".
   The shop's crossed price appears only on the product page, as "preț de referință afișat de magazin".
6. `make verify M=15` prints OK (the e2e uses a throwaway console observed at 2.000 lei for 41 days,
   then 1.700 lei: "−15% față de minimul din 30 de zile" and "Minim istoric").
