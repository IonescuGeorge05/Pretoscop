M25 — 5-minute founder check (promoted placements, merchant billing, optional ad slot)
Everything is off by default: flags `promoted.enabled` and `ads.slots.enabled`, no Stripe keys, no
AdSense id. On the dev database (or staging) without a Stripe account:
1. /admin/flaguri → turn on `promoted.enabled`. As an approved test merchant, open
   /pentru-magazine/cont → "Promovează ofertele": tick the box (text `promotedTerms.v1`) and
   activate. Without Stripe keys the page says card payment is not available: credit the wallet
   in /admin/promovare → Solduri (e.g. `150`, motiv "test") — the ledger shows "Ajustare".
2. Create a campaign: a category the merchant sells in, bid `1,00`, daily cap `2,00`. Open one of
   its products: a "Promovat" box sits above the offers list, says the shop pays for it and, when
   the promoted offer is not the cheapest, by how much it costs more. The first row of the list is
   still the cheapest offer, in the same order as before the campaign.
3. Click "Vezi oferta la …" in the box: you land on the shop's own page (no affiliate link).
   Click again from the same browser: not billed (duplicate). The merchant page shows the spend,
   the billed clicks and the cost per click (with one merchant it is the floor, 0,60 lei for
   1.000–2.999,99 lei); /admin/promovare shows the click and lets you refund it.
4. With Stripe (after D3, test-mode keys on staging): top up 150 lei from the merchant page; the
   Stripe page shows 150 lei + VAT (`TOPUP_VAT_PERCENT`); the balance grows only when Stripe's
   webhook arrives (never from the redirect). /admin/promovare → "Pentru contabil" → CSV gives
   net, VAT and gross per merchant for the invoices.
5. Read /cum-functioneaza#promovat, /cum-castigam-bani#promovare and /pentru-magazine/termeni#promovare
   (section 5): the rules match what you just saw. Before switching promotion on in production,
   email listed merchants the new terms 15 days ahead (P2B art. 3; docs/13 P5).
6. Ads stay off: a production build gets Google's ad origins into its CSP only with the GitHub
   variable `ADSENSE_CLIENT` set (D5, and a certified consent platform first: docs/13 P6). Then
   `ads.slots.enabled` shows a "Publicitate" box outside the offers table that asks for consent
   before loading anything.
