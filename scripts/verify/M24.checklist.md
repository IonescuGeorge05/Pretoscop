M24 — 5-minute founder check (affiliate operations)
1. Read `packages/core/src/affiliate.ts`: an affiliate link is used only when the program is
   approved, allows comparators (2Performant "Price Comparison Services" = yes, or a written OK),
   the URL is on the shop's own domain and the shop is not eMAG (ADR-013).
2. After the 2Performant account exists (A6/A7): set TWOPERFORMANT_EMAIL, TWOPERFORMANT_PASSWORD
   and TWOPERFORMANT_UNIQUE_CODE in .env, restart the worker; the next night `affiliate.sync`
   fills /admin/venituri → "Programe de afiliere". Check one program's permission against its page.
3. Turn on the flag `affiliate.links.enabled` (/admin/flaguri) only then. On a product with an
   approved shop, the offer shows "Link afiliat"; "Vezi oferta" goes through /out and lands on the
   shop (the 2Performant click appears in its dashboard within its reporting delay).
4. /admin/venituri shows yesterday's clicks, commissions, EPC; "Pentru contabil" gives the monthly
   totals per network (CSV).
5. A network report can be imported by hand: `pnpm --filter @pretoscop/worker commissions:import
   --network 2performant --file report.csv --dry-run` (then without --dry-run).
